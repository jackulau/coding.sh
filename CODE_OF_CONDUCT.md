# Code of conduct

coding.sh is a small project and we want it to stay a good place to ask questions, report bugs and
send patches, whatever your experience level.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most bad reports are just missing context.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Keep it on topic in issues and pull requests.
- If someone tells you a comment landed badly, take it seriously and adjust.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, including captured sessions, without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you
represent the project.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue, pull request or discussion (the
`···` menu). The report goes to the maintainer privately, and you will get a response within a few
days. For anything GitHub itself should see, such as spam or harassment across repositories, use
[GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Security problems are not conduct reports: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the project. The
response depends on what happened: usually a private note first, then a temporary or permanent ban
for repeated or serious problems.
